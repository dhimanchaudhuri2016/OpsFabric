ILLUSTRATIVE WORKFLOW / AGREED ESTATE SCOPE

A new advisory.
A focused response.

Turn an external risk signal into an asset-specific investigation. Start with discovery and lifecycle context, then focus attention on the services and devices that need a closer look.

  1. 01

    Know what is in scope

    Connect discovered assets, versions, configuration and service dependencies with the supported estate.

  2. 02

    Prioritise what matters

    Match advisory and lifecycle context to assets. Use service criticality and exposure to focus investigation.

  3. 03

    Make the next action clear

    Give the operational owner an evidence-backed assessment and a planned next check. Keep risk identification separate from a confirmed incident cause.

ILLUSTRATIVE DIAGNOSTIC JOURNEY

An application slows.
The investigation accelerates.

Replace repeated manual evidence gathering with a consistent diagnostic path. Collect the facts, test hypotheses and direct the right resolver to the next useful action.

  1. 01

    Trace the service path

    Use topology and dependencies to select relevant, non-invasive checks across the network path.

  2. 02

    Compare expected and observed

    Gather path, routing, health and configuration evidence through repeatable runbooks. Separate known facts from hypotheses.

  3. 03

    Act, then verify

    Pass findings to the appropriate team, trigger authorised workflows and check service health after the response.

DEVELOPMENT DIRECTION / TIMING TO BE CONFIRMED

Assisted troubleshooting.
Updates customers understand.

Gen AI assistance should help an operator troubleshoot with evidence and translate technical findings into clear customer language: what is affected, what is known, what is being done and when the next update is due.

See the roadmap direction
  1. 01

    Ground the response

    Use the relevant inventory, incident evidence, diagnostic results and approved knowledge as context.

  2. 02

    Assist troubleshooting

    Explain diagnostic findings, suggest the next checks and help build an investigation plan. Keep supporting evidence, uncertainties and untested hypotheses visible.

  3. 03

    Draft updates in customer language

    Prepare a plain-language update covering service impact, known facts, actions under way and the agreed next update. A person reviews it before sending; unknown facts and recovery times are never invented.

Operational examples are illustrative. Available tooling, integrations and automation depend on the agreed service and estate. Gen AI assistance is shown as a development direction, not a committed release.

A CLEARER PICTURE

Your questions. Connected.

How does Ops Fabric differ from Global Fabric?+

Global Fabric provides on-demand global network connectivity. Ops Fabric focuses on operational capabilities, service visibility, automation and assurance. Discuss how each fits your service estate with BT.

Can Ops Fabric support my existing estate?+

BT describes Ops Fabric as designed to integrate with existing tools, platforms and technology domains. Its flexible service catalogue lets you tailor observability, automation and operational support to your requirements.

How should we measure operational value?+

Start with a clear outcome and baseline. Agree service and operational measures for the selected capabilities, then review adoption and evidence before expanding.

Does Ops Fabric support global operations?+

Yes. BT’s official overview describes support for organisations spanning multiple sites, technologies and regions, backed by global Operational Hubs and experienced operational teams.
