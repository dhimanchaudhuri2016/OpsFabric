A CLEARER PICTURE

Your questions. Connected.

What is BT Ops Fabric?+

Ops Fabric brings together BT International’s operational capabilities across observability, experience, automation and assurance to support enterprise digital resilience.

How does Ops Fabric differ from Global Fabric?+

Global Fabric provides on-demand global network connectivity. Ops Fabric focuses on operational capabilities, service visibility, automation and assurance. Discuss how each fits your service estate with BT.

Can Ops Fabric support my existing estate?+

The appropriate capabilities depend on your technologies, services and operational requirements. A discovery conversation can identify your starting point and the scope of a suitable solution.

How should we measure operational value?+

Start with a clear outcome and baseline. Agree service and operational measures for the selected capabilities, then review adoption and evidence before expanding.
